# P-005 生成用提示词（按人物参考补全）

Use the attached three-view character reference exactly. Preserve the same young adult East Asian man across every frame: straight black slightly tousled hair, rectangular glasses, same face and jawline, average slim build, charcoal gray short-sleeve henley shirt, dark navy athletic shorts with white side panels, white crew socks, and light gray running shoes. Do not change his face, hairstyle, glasses, body proportions, or clothing.

Generate one continuous 5-second vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 30 fps, captured entirely by the iPhone FRONT selfie camera. The camera is the phone; every frame remains the front-camera point of view. Ordinary lived-in apartment living room in soft daytime window light, light wood floor, subtle background clutter. Natural straight-out-of-iPhone exposure and color, wide 24 mm equivalent selfie perspective, slight barrel distortion, lifted shadows, occasional autofocus and auto-exposure hunting, mild sensor noise and realistic motion blur. No beauty filter, skin smoothing, cinematic color grade, captions, logos, or camera UI. No cuts, no external camera angle, and no switch to the rear camera.

0.0–3.0s: He casually holds the phone at arm's length and idly records himself, framed chest-up with his head slightly high and off-center. He looks at the screen, blinks, shifts his weight, and gives a faint distracted half-smile. His free arm rests naturally. Only subtle irregular handheld micro-shake; never smooth or stabilized.

3.0–3.6s: His grip slips. Keep the shot continuous as the phone falls from his hand. The front-camera view tumbles rapidly; ceiling, wall, his arm and torso sweep through frame. Use physically plausible gravity, brief rolling-shutter skew, strong natural motion blur, and a short exposure jump toward the bright ceiling. The camera remains the phone's front camera throughout.

3.6–4.2s: The phone strikes the light wood floor with a hard, brief 2–3-frame impact shake and one small settle. It lies at a low tilted angle, still using the front camera. Show the underside of his chin near the top edge, his legs and shoes, and part of the ceiling; slightly soft focus. He bends down and reaches toward the lens, hand growing larger and blurred.

4.2–5.0s: His hand grabs and lifts the phone. The front-camera view swings up quickly past floor and legs, then settles slightly tilted and too close to his face. Autofocus hunts briefly and snaps onto his eyes. He looks mildly annoyed but amused, exhales, and holds still for the final moment.

Audio, if supported: quiet room tone, faint breathing and fabric rustle, a realistic plastic phone clatter on wood, a small breath reaction, then a light scrape as he picks it up. No music.

Negative prompt: different person, identity drift, face morphing, changing glasses or clothing, extra fingers, malformed hands, multiple people, floating phone, impossible fall physics, mirror-flip errors, rear camera, third-person camera, cuts, gimbal stabilization, cinematic lighting, slow motion, beauty filter, plastic skin, excessive bokeh, text, watermark, subtitles, logo, letterbox, CGI, cartoon, anime.
